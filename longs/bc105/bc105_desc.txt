It's the first of July, 1916, 7:30 a.m. A whistle blows. Before we get there: 13 real days from human history, and you wake up as an ordinary local in each one. Minutes, hours, days... or would you be fine?

In this video, we discuss:
- Why Egypt's tomb builders had sick days, beer and the first recorded strike
- What actually killed people at Pompeii in AD 79
- Why a gladiator on the Colosseum's opening day would probably live
- The ship that brought the Black Death to England in 1348, and smallpox in Tenochtitlan in 1520
- How the Mary Rose sank in 1545, and why the lower gun deck at Trafalgar was the safest place in the battle
- Why in Edo your poop was worth money, and why the Great Stink of 1858 didn't kill anyone
- The final tier list of all 13 days
