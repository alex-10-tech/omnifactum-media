A mapmaker drew a door in the King's castle that does not exist. Now she has one day, one candle and one very large cellmate to survive seven trials before the midnight bell.

In this episode:
- a lock that counts, and a spike that doesn't miss
- a word that tells you what the King does to traitors
- two guards, one gossip, and a 24-minute window
- a room that doubles its water every minute
- the price of light: what Wren has to burn
- a password that changes everything
- twelve bells, eleven seconds

Every trial is fair: all the clues are on screen before the pause. Pause, solve, comment your answer before the reveal.
